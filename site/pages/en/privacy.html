When you open the pages, the web server writes what is technically needed into its logs: your IP address, date and time, the page or file requested, the status code and the amount of data sent, the referrer, and your browser and operating system (user agent) — only to deliver the pages, keep them running safely and fend off faults and attacks (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The logs are deleted after 14 days at most, unless a specific security incident requires keeping them longer.

What these pages do not do

  • They set no cookies and keep nothing on your device but a note in your browser's session (sessionStorage) that the opening animation has run. It goes when you close the tab and never leaves your browser.
  • They load no fonts, scripts, images or videos from other servers.
  • They count no visits and show no advertising.
