Close a pane, or end a background agent.

/close — closes this pane (never the main one); while it works, a second /close stops it and closes it
/close <name> — ends that background agent, its turn stopped
/close <n> — closes pane n from where you are, by its number, name or role; you stay in your pane
/close alone, with more than this pane to close — a list of them, this pane first: ⏎ closes the one chosen

The session is kept for /resume. A pane in a Worktree asks how the Worktree ends: keep it and its branch, take it over (merge it into the branch you are on) or discard it. ⌃X 0 closes this pane too.
