Keep the project's to-dos in its `TODO.md`, a checklist you and Glut both use.

/todo — the To-dos tab: Space ticks an item, a adds one, o opens the editor
/todo <text> — adds an item at the list's end, while Glut works too
/todo open — the list in your editor
/todo clean — drops the done items, asked first

The rest of the file is never touched. ⌃Y shows or hides the tab.
