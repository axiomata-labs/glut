Go back to before one of your messages: the conversation, the files, or both.

/rewind — your messages, newest first; ⏎ on one asks what goes back: conversation and files, the conversation only, the files only, or a fork (a new session from there). ⎋⎋ opens the same list.

Nothing is lost: what the rewind cuts off stays as a session of its own under this one, “(before rewind)” in /resume, and the folder as it was is kept with it. Opening that session asks once whether its files come back too. The files come from Glut's snapshots, so what a command changed goes back as well as an edit; the folder as it was just before is kept aside first (`glut undo`). Past a compaction only the files can go back.
