Choose Glut's colours.

/theme — the themes to pick from
/theme <name> — glut, Glut's own, or one of Axiomata's themes (catppuccin-mocha, github-dark, …)

Glut colours only the text and keeps the terminal's background: a light theme wants a light terminal. Kept in `~/.glut/prefs.json`; in a window a host app opened, the choice holds for that session.

Each theme gives the footer's parts, the tools' names by their kind (reading, writing, commands, what reaches out), the panes and agents and the line above the input its own muted colours; what needs you stays bright. The reader mode and `NO_COLOR` keep it plain.
