Review what the last turn changed, hunk by hunk.

/diff — the changes of the last turn that changed files
/diff session — every change of this session

In the review: ↑↓ a hunk, ←→ a file, a accepts, r rejects (the hunk goes back, and Glut reads why with your next message), c comments (sent with your next message), v marks a file reviewed, s switches between turn and session, ⎋ closes. ⌃R opens it too; /review is the same command.
