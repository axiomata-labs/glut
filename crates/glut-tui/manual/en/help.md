Every command and every key, in a box of their own.

/help — the commands with a line each; typing filters them, ↑↓ choose, ⏎ opens the whole page of one
/help <command> — that command's page at once, as /help loop

On a page ↑↓ and PgUp PgDn scroll, ⎋ goes back to the list and closes the box from there. The last entry holds every key by topic, !cmd and the vi mode; ⌃K keeps the keys in the side panel.
