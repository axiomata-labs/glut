See the steps that changed files, and put files back.

/steps — every step that changed a file; ⏎ on one shows what it changed, or puts the files back to before it

Putting files back takes your edits since with them; the conversation stays. Glut keeps a snapshot before each changing step; what the files were just before is kept too, so `glut undo` brings it back. ⎋⎋ or /rewind rewinds the conversation instead.
